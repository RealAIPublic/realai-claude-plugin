# Reconciling documents and RealAI data

Use when documents are part of the task. Reconcile the figures needed for the question;
an uploaded rent roll does not automatically commission a diligence report.

## Establish what each source represents

Record source, date, period, units, scope, and whether a figure is actual, modeled, or
projected. A current PM export is usually strongest for current property operations;
a broker pro forma contains assumptions. Appraisals and third-party reports support facts
within their scope and date. A user model may contain both historical actuals and future
assumptions—inspect it rather than labeling every cell unreliable.

## Resolve material differences

- **Physical facts:** compare definitions and dates. Unit count may distinguish total,
  rentable, occupied, or permitted units; area may be gross or rentable. Use the best
  supported value and identify an unresolved material disagreement.
- **Current operations:** prefer reliable, recent operating records for the property over
  aggregate or modeled benchmarks. Check internal ties, accounting basis, concessions,
  and periods. A PM report is not immune from errors because it is recent.
- **Market context:** compare like-for-like comps and observed dates. RealAI's consistent
  coverage is useful, but source fitness and recency determine evidentiary weight; a
  datamart value does not automatically override a documented transaction.
- **Projections:** retain user assumptions as assumptions. Test material ones against
  evidence and show sensitivity where useful. Do not substitute them for observed actuals
  or require blanket confirmation before making progress on a preliminary analysis.

Do not silently average incompatible values or choose the more favorable source. State
which value supports the calculation and why when the difference matters. If the conflict
would change the result and cannot be resolved, ask or give conditional results.

Frame differences neutrally: a timing or definition mismatch is not necessarily an error.
A shared source note can cover several figures. Surface material conflicts, not a ledger
of every minor discrepancy or NULL benchmark field.
